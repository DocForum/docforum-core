Phase 8: active guardians can read and act on a dependent's consultations, referrals and lab results through an optional `patientProfileId` parameter on `GET /consultations/:id`, `GET /referrals/mine`, referral accept/decline and `GET /orders/lab-orders/:id/result` ([issue #305](https://github.com/DocForum/docforum-core/issues/305)). Every path authorises through `patientsService.resolveActingProfile`, ends immediately on revocation or at majority with `403 GUARDIANSHIP_INACTIVE`, and PHI-access-logs guardian reads. Existing caller-only behaviour is unchanged when the parameter is omitted; no schema changes.
